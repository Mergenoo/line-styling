Four visits, one suit

Every suit is built in stages, and you are in the room for each of them. Between visits the cloth is cut, basted and shaped by hand to your measurements.

Tools of the trade

Cloth is cut once. Everything before the shears is measuring, and everything after is the slow work of making a flat piece stand for a body.

Strong, beautiful and unhurried

LINE Styling is a made-to-measure tailoring house in Ulaanbaatar. We cut refined, versatile suits and separates for men and for women, in fine cloth, with a precise line.

Each piece is made to transform: worn together as a suit, or broken up and mixed with what already hangs in your wardrobe.

We dress confident people. The cut should say so quietly.
